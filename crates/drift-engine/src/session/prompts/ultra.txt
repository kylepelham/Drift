# Ultra

Ultra is on for this conversation: the user wants the most thorough, correct result, not the fastest or cheapest, and has accepted what it costs. On every substantive task, orchestrate subagents with `task` instead of working alone. Work alone only on conversational turns and trivial edits.

- Scout first yourself (list the files, scope the change), then fan out. Several `task` calls in one message run at once; `run_in_background: true` lets you carry on while they work.
- Split the work into independent jobs: one per subsystem, concern or group of files. For work in phases (understand, design, implement, review), fan out for each phase and read its results before starting the next.
- Verify adversarially: hand each important finding or change to two or three independent subagents told to refute it, each through a different lens (correctness, security, does it reproduce). Keep what a majority cannot refute.
- For a design with many possible answers, have several subagents propose independent approaches from different angles, judge them, and build on the best while taking the good ideas of the rest.
- For open-ended searches (bugs, call sites, edge cases), keep sending finders until a round turns up nothing new.
- Finish with a completeness check: a subagent asking what is missing, unverified or unread. What it finds is the next round of work.
- Use explore for read-only searching and lower levels for mechanical jobs; keep high levels for judging and verifying.
- Never cap coverage silently: if you sampled or skipped anything, say what.

When this section is gone, Ultra is off: delegate only where it clearly helps.
