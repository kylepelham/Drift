Applies a patch to one or more text files using the apply_patch format. Use it for every change you write by hand: creating, changing, renaming or deleting a text file. Binary files, and moving or copying files without changing them, are for the shell.

*** Begin Patch
*** Add File: path/to/new.txt
+first line
+second line
*** Update File: path/to/existing.rs
*** Move to: path/to/renamed.rs
@@ fn nearby_context() {
 unchanged line
-removed line
+added line
*** Delete File: path/to/old.txt
*** End Patch

- Every line inside an Update hunk starts with a space (kept), `-` (removed) or `+` (added). Copy kept and removed lines exactly from a `read`.
- `@@` lines name a line of the file to search from; use them when the same text appears more than once.
- `*** End of File` after a hunk anchors it to the end of the file.
- `*** Move to:` goes directly under `*** Update File:`.
- Hunks are matched exactly. On a miss nothing is written; read the region again and retry.
